01

Sleep — bed frame

Height under the bed is the whole game here

Read this before you shop beds at Target. Target currently has no good queen bed with built-in drawers from a brand worth trusting. Novogratz — the one reputable line that made them — has pulled its storage beds; only two Novogratz beds remain on the site and neither has storage. What's left in "queen storage bed" search results is marketplace dropship stock: no-name brands, 3-review listings, and $599-slashed-to-$149 pricing. IKEA genuinely wins this category.

So the play at Target is different, and honestly it's the more durable one: buy a tall solid-wood platform bed and let cheap under-bed bins do the storage work. No drawer glides to break, bins cost $15 each, and you can reconfigure whenever. The picks below are chosen on under-bed clearance.

Reviewers consistently call it solid and quiet, and at 59.4 lb one person can wrestle the box out of an elevator.

Buying it in NYC — practical notes

Pick the delivery tier carefully — you cannot change it after ordering. Target offers three: To-the-Door (free, left outside the unit), Inside-the-Door ($15–$150, scheduled, adult signature required), and Room of Choice ($40–$200, includes up/down stairs). Everything on this list is light enough — heaviest is the 200 lb sleeper sofa, then the mattress at 80 lb — that free To-the-Door is the sensible choice for most of it.

Call the carrier before the building calls you. Target's furniture carrier is EFW: 1-855-633-9669, target@efwnow.com. Most Manhattan buildings require a certificate of insurance from the delivery company and a booked service-elevator window. Target's own guidance is to communicate apartment restrictions directly to the carrier, so do that the same day you order.

Assembly is not included in any tier. Target sells it separately through Handy at about $69 per medium item — poor value on this list, since the desk top ships pre-assembled, the sofa is tool-free, the mirror and lamp need no assembly, and the table and chairs ship with the tools. The mattress and bed frame are the only two-person jobs.

Returns are unusually generous on Target's own brands. Threshold, Casaluna and Room Essentials carry a full year. Everything else is 90 days — with one trap: the Ashley Chime mattress is only 30 days. Check the Shipping & Returns block on each listing before buying.

Get a Target Circle Card before checkout. It's an extra 5% off (about $98 on this cart), free 2-day shipping, and 30 extra days on returns. Free Target Circle membership alone gets some of the deal pricing. Target Circle 360 is $99/year — but only $4.99/month for verified students, and a recent grad may still qualify; there's a 14-day free trial that covers a single furnishing spree nicely.
