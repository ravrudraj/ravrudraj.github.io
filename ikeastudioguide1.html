Buying it in NYC — practical notes for 20 Exchange Place

Delivery, not car. Order everything online in one cart from IKEA Brooklyn (Red Hook); flat-rate home delivery is far easier than hauling boxes downtown. The MALM bed alone is 4 packages at ~60 lb each.

Building rules first. 20 Exchange Place is a large doorman high-rise — before scheduling, ask the management office about delivery windows, freight elevator booking, and whether they require a Certificate of Insurance (COI) from the delivery company. IKEA's carriers can usually provide one, but it needs arranging in advance.

Assembly. Budget ~$150–250 for TaskRabbit assembly of the bed + sofa (bookable in the IKEA checkout). The desk, chair, and dining set are easy one-person jobs for a software engineer with an allen key.

Join IKEA Family (free) before checkout. It unlocks member pricing (the BRIMNES bed's $100 discount, for example) and a 90-day low-price guarantee.

Returns are generous: 365 days on furniture, and mattresses get a 90-day comfort exchange — so the VALEVÅG is a low-risk call.
